Greek is in % of notional per unit of its own factor; the matrix shows the move of that factor at the selected quantile over the MPOR.

P&L = Δ·ds + ½Γ·ds² + ν·dv + ρ·dr + Θ·h on 100,000 scenarios. Spot, volatility and rates are drawn independent; the volatility shock is lognormal so implied vol stays positive. The tail is identified on the total P&L and the same mask is applied to every leg, which is why the component figures add up to the total exactly.
